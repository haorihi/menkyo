## 2024-05-18 - Icon-Only Button Accessibility and Focus
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons used for primary navigation and actions (like Home, Bookmark, Export) in this specific design language require localized Japanese `aria-label`s for screen reader support and explicit focus rings (`focus-visible:outline-none focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-ink`) to ensure keyboard accessibility. These elements lack inherent semantic text and default visible focus indicators, making them completely inaccessible to keyboard and screen reader users otherwise.
**Action:** Always verify that every icon-only button contains a localized `aria-label` attribute describing its function and includes the standard Tailwind `focus-visible` ring utility classes to ensure a consistent, accessible experience.
